What you are actually paying for

Business insurance is not “just a fire policy”. It is a structure that protects property, liability and, where it matters, loss of profit if the business has to stop. The mistake we see most often is declaring the sums insured roughly and chasing only the lowest price.

With underinsurance, a large claim can be reduced in proportion. With over-insurance you pay more without a matching benefit. Deductibles can lower the premium, but you should know what share of each loss you keep.
